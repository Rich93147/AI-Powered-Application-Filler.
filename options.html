Contact details & address

Save your address once, whether you live in a house, apartment, dorm, or another residence. Address line 2 is optional; leave it blank if it doesn't apply. This information stays in Firefox local storage.

Eligibility & self-identification

Use your confirmed answers. For veteran or disability status, selecting Yes will require review on the application.

Countries of citizenship

Add every country where you currently hold citizenship. Citizenship is separate from residence, nationality/ethnicity, and birthplace.

Choose your role profile

Role focus

Save the fundamentals once. Ollama decides which projects and details fit each application question.

Resume document

Upload a resume once. Text-based PDFs can be read locally; image-only/scanned PDFs are not supported.

Read resume & extract details

Read text directly from a saved, text-based PDF or TXT resume, or paste it below. No cloud service is used. A running local Ollama model extracts facts into a reviewable draft. Projects are not imported as employment, and existing details are preserved. Scanned/image-only PDFs are not supported; DOC/DOCX can be attached but are not parsed.

Education

Leave GPA and unknown information blank. School suggestions come from ROR; typing a search sends only the school name query to that service.

Work experience

Only employment and internships belong here. Academic and personal projects are kept in Projects, even if they appear under an Experience heading on your resume.

Projects

Keep the complete story of each project in one place. Ollama will adapt it to the question and character limit.

Optional additional inputs

Extra context if you want it. None of these fields are required, and they never override the wording of a specific job application.

Local AI can suggest optional context from confirmed facts. Review before saving.

Application history

Opened, in-progress, and completed applications are tracked by the specific job listing. An open page is never treated as proof of submission.

Each tab has its own list. Favorites can include jobs from any stage.

Completed requires confirmation of submission, not just a finished form. Star a listing to keep it in Favorites without changing its status. Deleting a listing also removes its favorite; clearing history does not affect your profiles.

Learned information

Answers learned after you review questions or continue through an employer's form. Check what was saved, edit mistakes, or remove answers you no longer want reused.

Answers with the same meaning are grouped within their section. School dates and employment dates are kept separate. Education details are also available under Role profiles.

Question meanings

Wordings you confirmed mean the same as a known question, so they share one saved answer. Remove one if it was wrong.

Questions to always skip

Optional questions you've chosen not to answer. Remove a rule to be asked again.

A saved answer is reused when ApplicationFiller recognizes the same question for the same role profile. Company-specific responses stay tied to their original listing. Some unfamiliar or materially different wording still requires review.

Automation

Automatic filling and intermediate navigation are always enabled until an answer needs attention or final submission is reached.

Local AI (Ollama)

Optional answer drafting and resume extraction run on your own computer. No OpenAI key or cloud account. Install Ollama, run ollama pull llama3.2:3b, then start Ollama locally. No applicant profile information is uploaded to a cloud AI service.

Ollama may require OLLAMA_ORIGINS=moz-extension://* (or your specific Firefox extension origin). Allowing the specific origin is more secure.
